WCAG 2.1 Contrast Matrix — AA & AAA

Paste your palette below. Contrast is computed with the exact WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula; pass/fail uses the unrounded ratio. AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (≥ 24px, or ≥ 18.66px bold); AAA requires 7:1 and 4.5:1.

1 · Colors

One color per line. Optional name before the value, e.g. primary: #0B5CAD. Accepts #RGB, #RRGGBB, rgb(r, g, b), or comma/space-separated hex codes.

3 · Contrast matrix — rows are backgrounds, columns are text colors

4 · Usable combinations (AA)
